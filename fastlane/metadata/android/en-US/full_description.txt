Kikidame ("kiki-dame", from the Japanese for "listen to what you have saved up") is for people who keep recorded radio programs on a Jellyfin server, a NAS share or a folder on their phone. It downloads the episodes you want to your phone, program by program, and plays them without needing the source. Podcast files work too.

What it does:

- Retention rules per program: choose "Keep latest N" and "Delete after played", and sync (every 3 hours, Wi-Fi only if you like) downloads what is missing and removes what is no longer needed. Episodes you download by hand are pinned and never removed by the rules. If a file on the source is rewritten later (for example when chapters are added), sync replaces the copy on your device, keeping your playback position.
- Offline first: playback always uses the files on your device. Playback position and played state are kept on the device, and playback does not stop when the server is unreachable (with a Jellyfin books library they are also shared with the server when it can be reached).
- Player: background playback with notification and lock-screen controls, skip 10 seconds back and forward, speed 1.0x to 2.0x, sleep timer (by time or until the end of the episode), a chapter list (chapters embedded in the file), resume where you left off, and automatic advance to the next episode of the same program.
- Android Auto: browse Continue / Starred / Programs on the car screen and play with the same rules as on the phone.
- English and Japanese UI, switchable per app on Android 13 and later.
- Open source under the MPL-2.0. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Not for: music (episodes do not pile up) or audiobooks (you listen from the oldest chapter, so "keep the latest N" makes no sense).

Requires one of these, depending on where your recordings are:

- Jellyfin 10.10 or later, with your audio in a books library or a music library (album = program, album artist = publisher). A books library is recommended: the playback position and played state are shared with Jellyfin Web and other devices signed in as the same user
- A NAS shared folder (SMB2/3), with a folder per publisher, a folder per program inside it, and the episode files inside that
- A folder on your device, organized the same way

And Android 12 or later.

Kikidame assumes one source at a time (one Jellyfin server, user and library, or one NAS shared folder or folder on the device), and, except with a Jellyfin books library, that this app is the only place you listen. Source and issues: https://github.com/t-seki/kikidame
